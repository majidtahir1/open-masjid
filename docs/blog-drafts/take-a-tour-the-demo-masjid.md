You can read every feature page we write, but the fastest way to know whether
software fits your masjid is to use it. So we built a real one and left the doors
open. **Masjid al-Noor** is a complete, always-on demo masjid at
[demo.openmasjid.app](https://demo.openmasjid.app) — prayer times, events,
donations, membership, the lobby screen, all of it — set up the way an actual
masjid would run it. Click around. Nothing here is a screenshot.

## Start where your community starts

Open the [public site](https://demo.openmasjid.app) first, because that is the
front door your families land on. You will see the prayer times with a live
iqamah countdown, the Jumu'ah announcement up top, and the upcoming programs a
real community runs: a Qur'an circle on Thursdays, Chess for Kids on Sundays,
the summer camp sign-up. Scroll down for the services and the Donate button.
Open it on your phone too. It is built mobile-first, because that is where most
of your congregation will actually read it.

## Get behind the desk

The public site is half the story. To see how a volunteer runs the place, log
into the admin dashboard. We do not publish the password — shared logins left
out on the open internet get abused — so email
[hello@openmasjid.app](mailto:hello@openmasjid.app) and we will send you the demo
credentials.

Once you are in, change a prayer time, post an announcement, turn a flyer into an
event, or build a signup form with a capacity limit. And if clicking through
menus is not your thing, just tell **Ansari**, the built-in assistant, what you
want done and watch it happen. That is the whole idea: a volunteer with ten
minutes should still be able to get something done.

## Yes, the money works too

This is the part most demos quietly fake. The demo runs the full payment layer in
Stripe's test mode, so donations (Sadaqah, Zakat, the Building Fund), the
membership tiers (Supporter, Family, Patron), and paid forms all work end to end
— checkout, receipt, and the new member showing up in the admin. Pay with the
test card `4242 4242 4242 4242`, any future expiry, any CVC. No real money ever
moves. It is the exact flow your treasurer would see, without the risk.

## Go ahead, be rough with it

Explore without holding back. Every night the demo wipes itself clean and
re-seeds, so anything you add, edit, or delete is gone by morning and back to a
tidy starting point. You cannot break it and you cannot leak anything — so poke
at the corners.

We built the demo because we have sat where you are sitting: trying to work out,
before committing the masjid to anything, whether a tool will actually make the

May Allah make the khidmah of your masjid easy, and accept it.
