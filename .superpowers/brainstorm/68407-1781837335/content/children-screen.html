A

Big cards

One large card + one big button per child. Fewer kids per family means whitespace is fine; huge touch targets, no mis-taps. Best for clarity.

B

Compact list + check-in-all

Denser, with a one-tap "check in all siblings." Faster for big families, but smaller targets and a checkbox concept parents must grok.

C

Hybrid

Big cards (A) PLUS a "Check in all" button at the top (from B). Best of both — recommended if families often have 3+ kids.
